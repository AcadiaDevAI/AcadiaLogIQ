1. Cisco TAC — Global Contact Directory

Cisco TAC is the primary escalation path for incidents involving Cisco hardware or software (Catalyst, Nexus, ASR/ISR, ASA/Firepower, Wireless LAN Controllers, Meraki, Webex, etc.). Use the regional number for your operating region; the web portal is preferred for non-P1 cases because it captures attachments cleanly and gives you a Service Request (SR) number immediately.

Cisco support entitlement is contract-driven. Confirm the customer's contract type before promising response SLAs to a stakeholder.

3.1 How to verify entitlement before calling

StepAction
1Pull device Serial Number from show inventory.
2On cisco.com → Support → Coverage Tools → Service Contract Center, enter the SN.
3If using a Smart Account, log in to software.cisco.com and confirm the Virtual Account holds the device.
4Capture: contract number, end-of-coverage date, replacement SLA, TAC entitlement (24×7 vs 8×5).
5Paste those four fields into the Stage 5 escalation package.

4. Customer Validation — NOC Engineer Playbook

Before any vendor SR is opened on a customer's behalf, the NOC must validate the requesting contact is authorised. This protects the customer from unauthorised configuration changes and protects Acadia from contract violations.

StageCheckSource of truth
IdentityCaller name matches an authorised contactCustomer CMDB / contract LOA
IdentityCallback number matches the customer's registered DIDCMDB / Salesforce account
AuthorisationCaller is on the 'Authorised To Open Tickets' listCustomer onboarding form
ScopeAffected device / circuit is in the customer's managed inventoryAsset register
SeveritySeverity selected matches business-impact wordingSeverity matrix in MSA
PrivacyConfirm consent before sharing data with 3rd-party vendor (GDPR / DPA)Customer Data Processing Agreement

4.1 Validation script (call-opening template)

"Hi, this is <NOC engineer> from Acadia NOC. Can you confirm your full name, the customer site you're calling about, and a callback number? I'll validate you against your authorised-contacts list before we proceed. Once validated, I'll open the case and share the SR number with you."
